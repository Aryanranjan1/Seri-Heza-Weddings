# **App Name**: Seri Heza Weddings

## Core Features:

- Homepage Hero Section: Visually striking full-viewport hero section with a cinematic wedding image, headline, subheading, and a 'Start Your Journey' call to action, creating an immediate emotional impact.
- Editorial Introduction Section: Asymmetrical layout with floating elements, including a bridal portrait, text block with a founder-led narrative, and a detail image to humanize the brand.
- Service Highlights: A four-column grid showcasing key services (Bridal Makeup, Pelamin & Décor, Busana & Attire, Full Wedding Planning) with circular or outlined icons and a clear 'Explore Full Services' CTA.
- Portfolio Display: A two-column masonry grid presenting real wedding images with couple names and links to explore each event, providing visual proof of luxury and event quality.
- SEO & Authority Section: Split-screen section with an FAQ accordion addressing SEO-driven questions and editorial articles linking to Malay wedding trends and advice.
- Conversion Section: Split conversion section with a dark maroon area featuring a WhatsApp CTA for immediate chats and a clean area for structured inquiries via form submission, ensuring lead capture.
- Footer: Minimal footer design containing copyright information, social media links, and the physical address, projecting quiet confidence and ensuring necessary contact details are available.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary background color: Warm cream (#F9F8F4) to evoke a sense of luxury and softness.
- Secondary background color: Pale dusty rose (#F2E8E4) to subtly highlight the SEO section.
- Primary text color: Deep charcoal (#2C2C2C) for a sophisticated and readable contrast.
- Accent color: Muted gold (#C5A059) used sparingly for dividers, hover states, and icons to provide elegant highlights.
- Conversion color: WhatsApp green (#25D366) exclusively for the WhatsApp CTA to make it instantly recognizable.
- Headings: 'Playfair Display' (serif) for hero headlines and section titles, loose line-height (1.2–1.3), regular/semi-bold weight. Note: currently only Google Fonts are supported.
- UI/Labels/Navigation: 'Montserrat' (sans-serif) in ALL CAPS with letter-spacing (+0.15em to +0.25em), light/regular weight for navigation, buttons, tags, and captions. Note: currently only Google Fonts are supported.
- Body Text: 'Lora' (serif) calm and readable, comfortable font size (16–18px desktop). Note: currently only Google Fonts are supported.
- Intentional asymmetry: Avoid traditional grids to ensure sections feel curated, not stacked, mirroring a high-end magazine layout.
- Large negative space: Maximize 'breathing room' to enhance the perception of luxury and sophistication, preventing a crowded feel.
- Vertical rhythm: Prioritize vertical arrangement and flow to guide the user experience effectively.
- Subtle scroll reveals: Implement fade or slide effects on scroll with slow, elegant transitions (300–500ms) for meaningful user interaction.
- Meaningful hover states: Apply hover effects exclusively to CTAs, links, and portfolio elements to enhance user interactivity without unnecessary animation.
